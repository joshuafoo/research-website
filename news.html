2025

  • 16th Dec. I gave an invited talk at RBM70, a conference celebrating Prof. Robert Mann’s contributions to physics.

  • 27th Nov. I accepted a position at the Institute for Advanced Study, Kyushu University, as an Inamori Associate Professor!
